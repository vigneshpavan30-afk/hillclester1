WHO WE ARE

Old-fashioned family care, delivered with modern dentistry.

Hillcrest Dental Studio is a family-owned practice in Chino Hills with a focus on hospitality and a one-on-one approach. We treat children, teens, adults and seniors under one roof, so families keep the same trusted team through every stage of life.

Our vision is to provide comprehensive care to all our patients, backed by the latest technology and the utmost integrity. That means honest treatment plans, clear pricing conversations before we begin, and no procedure you don't need.
